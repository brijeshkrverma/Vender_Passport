Understanding Your Scorecard

Each focus area is assessed through a set of questions, with each question scored out of 100 marks. The final score is calculated as follows:
  • Scaling Focus Area Scores: Each focus area score is adjusted to a 100-point scale.
  • Applying Weightage: Each focus area is assigned an equal weightage of 20%.
  • Final Score Calculation: The weighted scores from all focus areas are summed to arrive at the final score, which is presented out of 100.
